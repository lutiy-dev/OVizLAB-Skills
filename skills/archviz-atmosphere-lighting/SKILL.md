# archviz-atmosphere-lighting
## Trigger
Use for direct realistic architectural light, weather, seasonal mood and environmental depth without altering architecture. Follow repository AGENTS.md; skills guide work and do not imply installed software or verified output.
## Inputs
Render references, location/climate assumptions, shot brief, allowable weather and vegetation changes.
## Procedure
1. Define emotional intent and physically plausible sun, sky, exposure and white balance relationships.
2. Plan separate controlled variants for golden hour, overcast, blue hour, rain or fog only when appropriate to project context.
3. Specify foreground/midground/background, haze, reflections, interior glow, people and vegetation as editable layers.
4. Compare against raw Corona render to detect fabricated windows, facade shifts and implausible shadows.
5. Deliver shot-specific lighting notes, pass requirements and repeatability settings.
## PASS / FAIL and reporting
Separate GENERATED, VALIDATED and TESTED. Report actual sources, versions, settings, evidence, observed render/export checks, unresolved risks and rollback. A planning document alone is not a tested film, image or scene.
## Stop conditions
PASS requires inspected variant renders and geometry check; STOP on contradictory illumination, unsupported season or altered architecture.
