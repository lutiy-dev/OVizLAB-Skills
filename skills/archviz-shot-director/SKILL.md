# archviz-shot-director
## Trigger
Use for develop camera and visual storytelling shot briefs for photorealistic exterior render production. Follow repository AGENTS.md; skills guide work and do not imply installed software or verified output.
## Inputs
3ds Max/Corona scene or references, client-approved architecture, site and visual goals.
## Procedure
1. Establish a view matrix: hero, eye-level, context, architectural detail and optional aerial.
2. Specify camera height, focal length target, two-point verticals, framing, sun direction and subject hierarchy as proposals, not guessed actual settings.
3. Review silhouette, edges, landscaping depth, scale cues and facade visibility; avoid distortion and architecture changes.
4. Give actionable shot briefs and A/B checkpoints, including what to inspect in test renders.
## PASS / FAIL and reporting
Separate GENERATED, VALIDATED and TESTED. Report actual sources, versions, settings, evidence, observed render/export checks, unresolved risks and rollback. A planning document alone is not a tested film, image or scene.
## Stop conditions
PASS requires approved shot list and inspected renders; STOP when proposed framing misrepresents required design or safety-critical access.
