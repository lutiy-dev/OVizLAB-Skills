# archviz-animation-director
## Trigger
Use for design architectural camera movement and cinematic shot sequences for unreal, 3ds max or rendered animation. Follow repository AGENTS.md; skills guide work and do not imply installed software or verified output.
## Inputs
Scene, intended duration/platform, shots, camera constraints, render budget and approved architecture.
## Procedure
1. Draft a shot-by-shot storyboard with one clear subject and transition rationale per shot.
2. Define camera path, speed, lens, easing, foreground occlusion and parallax; prioritize legibility over movement for movement's sake.
3. Plan day/night or weather continuity and conservative object/vegetation animation where applicable.
4. Prepare render passes and frame-range notes for postproduction; protect spatial continuity and building proportions.
5. Review low-resolution playblasts/animatics before costly final rendering.
## PASS / FAIL and reporting
Separate GENERATED, VALIDATED and TESTED. Report actual sources, versions, settings, evidence, observed render/export checks, unresolved risks and rollback. A planning document alone is not a tested film, image or scene.
## Stop conditions
PASS requires observed animatic and shot continuity check; STOP on clipping, impossible motion, unapproved geometry or unverified performance.
